03 / STRONG FIT · CYBERNETICS

The Architectural Relevance of Gordon Pask

Haque’s account of Pask treats environments as participants in ongoing conversations. That is a close match for asking how architecture might learn from non-human users rather than only act on their behalf. The supplied excerpt specifically describes shared environments and participants learning from one another.

Usman Haque, “The Architectural Relevance of Gordon Pask,” Architectural Design 77, no. 4 (2007). Verify page range and issue details against the supplied reading.

04 / STRONG FIT · MODELS & REPRESENTATION

The Inner Life of Models

Wiscombe and Trotter argue that models are shaped by deliberate choices about scale, material, and detail. That makes this reading especially useful for examining how a design model can make some users’ needs legible while leaving others out—the core challenge in representing non-human futures.

Tom Wiscombe and Marrikka Trotter, “The Inner Life of Models” (2020). Supplied course PDF; verify publication venue and pagination against the published text.

DESIGN & EDITORIAL DECISIONS

Human direction

The group described a visual card pile, search and taxonomy filters, slide-out reading details, citations, and a public-facing learning goal. It then specified the interaction: selecting one card should lift it from a slightly blurred pile, show three related readings from different categories for cross-pollination, and keep the detail drawer on the right. The group later asked for the pile to shuffle on each page load so the same cards would not always sit on top. It explicitly chose not to host PDFs, asking instead for information, quotations, and citations. The group rejected the speculative “Working position” copy and an earlier cybernetics configurator panel as confusing; it subsequently supplied a separate assignment brief for a more complete configurator.

AI response

The app keeps cards, search, filters, the selected-card view, cross-category recommendations, and the detail drawer; the pile order is randomized once per page load and remains stable while visitors search or filter. The rejected argument and earlier panel remain removed. The new configurator is a separate, assignment-directed feature. No PDF upload or hosting is included. This records the group’s design choices rather than attributing them to AI.

Three documented decisions and corrections
