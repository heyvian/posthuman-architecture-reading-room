What the rules mean—and where they come from

The feedback-loop structure is the cybernetics idea being tested: sense a condition, compare it with a goal, act, then sense again. The controls are the group’s simplified model choices. They are not measurements or claims from either pitch link. The exact mapping must be checked against the group’s primary and scholarly sources before final submission.

Source-derived idea Feedback and regulation as a loop.Our assumptions 20 °C start; 24 steps; fixed disturbance; correction cap; simplified thermal response.What is left out Multiple species, conflicting goals, spatial differences, adaptation, and real building physics.
